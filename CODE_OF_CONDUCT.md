# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the AITIR community a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive, evidence-grounded feedback;
- distinguishing technical disagreement from personal criticism;
- acknowledging mistakes and taking responsibility;
- protecting credentials, personal data, confidential telemetry, and sensitive security information;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the safety of affected people.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, unwanted attention, or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- knowingly submitting fabricated evidence, credentials, personal telemetry, or unsafe exploit details through public channels;
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Project standards

AITIR discussions must preserve the repository's evidence and safety boundaries. Participants must not represent synthetic data as production evidence, submitted work as accepted, standards mappings as certification, or model output as response authority.

Security vulnerabilities and sensitive disclosures must use GitHub's private security-advisory mechanism rather than a public issue.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. They may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are inconsistent with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately through the maintainer contact identified in [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff). Report sensitive security matters through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/sajjad47/AITIR-Framework/security/advisories/new).

All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters to the extent reasonably possible.

Enforcement may include a private correction, formal warning, temporary restriction, permanent removal from project spaces, or referral to platform authorities, depending on context and severity.
