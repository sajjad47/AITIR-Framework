## Relationship to Version 2

Version 2 synthesizes interfaces and safeguards across the four research modules. It does not aggregate their numerical findings into a single efficacy claim. The architecture intentionally labels:

- standards-derived requirements;
- Version 2 architectural propositions;
- published prior findings;
- submitted-study findings;
- reproduced repository checks;
- future operational evidence.

## Repository artifact

The Version 2 repository provides schemas, documentation, a 12-row synthetic conformance example, a validator, diagrams, and public PDFs. It does not include the licensed/raw datasets or every implementation artifact from the submitted studies.

## Research priorities

- prospective, lawful, multi-organization identity telemetry evaluation;
- measured analyst review accuracy and workload under abstention;
- calibrated relation confidence and local disruption cost for identity graphs;
- response-authority policies reviewed by real mission, legal, privacy, continuity, and security owners;
- adversarial testing of telemetry, models, threat feeds, identity resolution, and generative-AI assistance;
- connector fault injection, evidence preservation, rollback, and service-continuity measurement;
- independent replication and control assessment.
