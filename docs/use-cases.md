# Public-Sector Use Cases

AITIR is designed for cybersecurity problems common in government and public-sector technology environments.

## Law-Enforcement Technology

Law-enforcement environments depend on sensitive records, investigative systems, secure communications, identity controls, endpoint security, remote access, and operational continuity. AITIR can help prioritize suspicious identity or access activity affecting systems used for criminal justice, public safety, records management, and interagency information sharing.

Example use cases:

- review of suspicious remote-access activity;
- prioritization of privileged access to sensitive systems;
- identification of abnormal authentication behavior;
- escalation of access events affecting mission-critical systems;
- audit-ready documentation of identity-related reviews.

## Digital Government Services

Digital government services require trust, continuity, and secure access for both staff and citizens. AITIR can support risk-based review of access events across service platforms, administrative portals, and identity-dependent workflows.

Example use cases:

- protecting citizen-service platforms from account misuse;
- prioritizing identity events affecting high-volume public services;
- reviewing access to administrative dashboards;
- supporting incident-response documentation.

## Public-Sector Infrastructure

Public agencies often operate distributed networks, endpoint fleets, remote users, cloud services, and legacy systems. AITIR can help connect identity events to infrastructure risk.

Example use cases:

- prioritizing access events involving critical servers;
- reviewing unusual administrator activity;
- correlating endpoint signals with account behavior;
- supporting business continuity and disaster recovery planning.

## Research and Training

AITIR can also serve as a research and training model for:

- identity-centered threat analysis;
- explainable risk scoring;
- cybersecurity awareness training;
- public-sector incident-response exercises;
- future publication and practitioner review.
